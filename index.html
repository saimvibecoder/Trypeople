Privacy

Your device, your contacts, your dashboard

Most apps that organise contacts upload the whole list to a server first. People reads them where they already are, on your phone, and keeps everything there.

  • Works offline. No internet needed to browse, search or add notes.
  • No servers. Contacts go straight from your phone, files or Google account to this device.
  • No account. Open it and it works. Nothing to sign up for.
