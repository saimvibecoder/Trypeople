Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

In short: People has no server. Everything you add, import or sync is stored only in your own browser on your own device. We never receive, see, sell or share your contacts.

What People is

People is a web app at trypeople.in that shows your contacts as a personal dashboard: who you're close to, who you haven't spoken to in a while, and reminders to keep in touch. It runs entirely in your web browser.

What is stored, and where

The following is saved in your browser's local storage on the device you are using, and nowhere else:

  • The name you ask People to greet you with.
  • Contacts you add by hand, import from a file (.vcf, Excel or CSV), pick from your phone, or share into the app: names, phone numbers, email addresses, cities, roles and notes.
  • Things you add yourself: favourites, reminders, "last spoken" dates and private notes.
  • Your settings, such as your country code.

Files you import are read inside your browser. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Google user data

If you choose Continue with Google, People asks Google only for your basic profile: your name and email address, so it can greet you and show which account is connected. People does not ask for access to your Google Contacts or any other Google data.

How this data is used:

  • It is sent directly from Google to your browser and used only to greet you inside People.
  • It is stored only in your browser on your device. It is never sent to us or to any other server, person or company.
  • People never changes or deletes anything in your Google account.
  • It is not used for advertising, sold, or used to train artificial intelligence models.

People's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect Google: in People, open Settings and tap Sign out. This revokes People's access. You can also remove access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete contacts: in People, tap Remove all above your people list, or use Settings.
  • Delete everything: clear this site's data in your browser settings, or uninstall the app.

Cookies and tracking

People does not use cookies for tracking, analytics or advertising. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Children

People is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
